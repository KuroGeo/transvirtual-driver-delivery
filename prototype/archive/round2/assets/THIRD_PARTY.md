# Third-party resources

- Carbon icons by IBM: https://github.com/carbon-design-system/carbon — Apache-2.0. Icons sourced through Iconify's Carbon collection: https://github.com/iconify/icon-sets/blob/master/json/carbon.json . Cached locally in `icons.json`, embedded in the generated HTML. No Carbon brand logo is used.
- Alpine.js: https://github.com/alpinejs/alpine — MIT. Vendored from the installed Oil UI skill's `assets/vendor/alpine.min.js`.
- Oil UI comparison-page generator: https://github.com/oil-oil/oil-ui — MIT. Generates `style-explorer.html` from `manifest.json`; locally adjusted default mobile viewport and scheme-number text contrast.

- Inter Variable by Rasmus Andersson: https://github.com/rsms/inter — SIL Open Font License 1.1. The font is vendored from Google Fonts; license in `Inter-OFL.txt`. Used by the round-two layout, served locally and embedded in the comparison artifact.

No network-loaded fonts, tracking, image-generation service, or paid asset service is used.
